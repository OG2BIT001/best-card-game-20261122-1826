EMBER CLASH
A duel of the four embers — Flame, Tide, Thorn and Stone

An original card duel: 14 original cards, four elements told apart by colour and shape, and Rook the Ember Warden, a rival who plays to win. Spend embers, summon creatures, guard your hero — and burn Rook's life to zero.

Your turn: you gain +1 max ember (up to 10), refill your embers and draw a card.
Play cards: click a card in your hand. Creatures join your side of the table; spells need a target — click an enemy creature or Rook himself.
Attack: click one of your rested creatures, then an enemy creature or Rook. Creatures rest the turn they arrive. Guard creatures must be destroyed before Rook can be struck.
Shield soaks damage before life does. First hero to 0 life loses.
Keys: 1–7 pick hand cards · Space ends your turn · Esc puts a card down.
